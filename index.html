🏥 Clinical Diagnostic Standards & Reference Thresholds

Validated clinical diagnostic boundaries used by our risk attribution engine to evaluate patient parameters.

🫀 ACC / AHA Cardiovascular Guidelines

  • Resting Blood Pressure:
    Normal: < 120 mmHg | Stage 1 HTN: 130–139 | Stage 2: ≥ 140
  • Serum Cholesterol:
    Desirable: < 200 mg/dL | Borderline: 200–239 | High: ≥ 240
  • Electrocardiographic ST Depression:
    Normal: < 1.0 mm | Clinically Significant Ischemia: ≥ 1.5 mm
  • Fluoroscopy Vessels (ca):
    0: Clear coronary arteries | > 0: Luminal arterial calcification

🩸 ADA Diabetes Standards of Care

  • Fasting Blood Glucose:
    Normal: < 100 mg/dL | Pre-diabetes: 100–125 | Diabetic: ≥ 126
  • Body Mass Index (BMI):
    Normal: 18.5–24.9 | Overweight: 25.0–29.9 | Obese: ≥ 30.0 kg/m²
  • Basal Serum Insulin:
    Physiological: 16–166 μU/mL | Hyperinsulinemia: > 166 μU/mL
  • Diabetes Pedigree Function:
    Low Genetic Risk: < 0.50 | Elevated Familial Predisposition: ≥ 0.60

🧠 Oxford Acoustic Vocal Pathology

  • Cycle-to-Cycle Jitter (%):
    Normal: < 0.60% | Phonatory Micro-Tremor: ≥ 0.70%
  • Cycle-to-Cycle Shimmer:
    Normal: < 0.03 | Acoustic Glottal Incompetence: ≥ 0.035
  • Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio (HNR):
    Clear Resonance: > 20 dB | Breathiness / Turbulence: < 18 dB
  • Pitch Period Entropy (PPE):
    Nominal: < 0.15 | Parkinsonian Vocal Entropy: ≥ 0.20
  • Nonlinear Dynamic Spread (spread1):
    Normal: ≤ -6.0 | Pathological Dispersion: > -5.0
